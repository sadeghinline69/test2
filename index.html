Automatic Speak: The Speak button is pressed automatically. While listening, it becomes blue. The microphone keeps listening until it recognizes a command. As soon as a command is recognized, it executes immediately. Each automatic listening turn executes at most one command, then the next listening turn starts automatically. Commands are recognized inside longer sentences, for example: “Please move right now.”
