Automatic Speak: The Speak button is pressed automatically. While listening, it becomes blue. The microphone listens for a maximum of 4 seconds. As soon as it recognizes a command, it executes it immediately and stops listening. Each automatic listening turn executes at most one command, then the next listening turn starts automatically. Commands are recognized inside longer sentences, for example: “Please go right now.”
Commands: “go right”, “go left”, “jump”, “stop”, “take the key”, “open the door”.
