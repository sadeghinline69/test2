How to Play

On your turn, do exactly one action: draw Train Car cards, claim one route, or draw Destination Tickets.

To claim a route, first tap a Train Card color in your hand, then tap the railway you want. If that railway cannot be claimed with the selected color, the game will warn you. Gray routes may use any single color. Locomotives are wild. With 2–3 players, only one route of a parallel pair may be claimed; with 4–5 players, both may be claimed, but by different players.

When any player ends a turn with 2 or fewer trains, every player (including that player) gets exactly one final turn. Then completed Destination Tickets add their printed value and incomplete ones subtract it. Route points: 1→1, 2→2, 3→4, 4→7, 5→10, 6→15.
